SEPE Appointment Guide 2026: How to Book Online, by Phone, or In Person

Booking a SEPE appointment is an important step for people in Spain who need assistance with unemployment benefits, employment-related procedures, or other services provided by the State Public Employment Service. Whether you are applying for unemployment benefits, asking about an existing claim, or preparing to visit an employment office, understanding the appointment process can help you save time and avoid unnecessary confusion.

In 2026, people can explore different ways to manage their SEPE appointments, including online booking, telephone assistance, and in-person visits when required. Choosing the right method depends on the type of procedure you need to complete and whether your application can be handled electronically. Preparing your personal information and relevant documents before starting can also make the process easier.

This complete SEPE appointment guide explains how to book an appointment online, which telephone number to use, what to expect during an office visit, and how to deal with common booking problems. It also explains when an appointment may not be necessary because certain procedures can be completed through the official SEPE electronic office.

What Is a SEPE Appointment?

SEPE stands for Servicio Público de Empleo Estatal, Spain's State Public Employment Service. It manages unemployment benefits and provides information and administrative services related to employment support.

A SEPE appointment, commonly known as cita previa SEPE, allows a person to arrange assistance with a procedure that requires attention from a SEPE benefits office. Depending on the circumstances, this may involve applying for unemployment benefits, discussing an existing claim, requesting information about a benefit, or completing another administrative procedure.

People often search for terms such as sepe cita previa, and SEPE appointment by phone when looking for instructions on arranging a visit. Understanding which service you need is the first step because not every employment-related procedure is handled by the same public authority.

SEPE mainly handles unemployment benefits and related matters. Registration as a job seeker, employment demand renewals, and certain job-search services are generally managed by the public employment service of the relevant autonomous community.

How to Book a SEPE Appointment Online

Booking online is a convenient option for people who want to arrange an appointment without making a telephone call. The official SEPE electronic office provides access to appointment services and information about available administrative procedures.

To begin, visit the official SEPE appointment.

Follow these general steps to request your appointment:

  1. Open the official SEPE website. Use the appointment section of the electronic office rather than an unfamiliar third-party website.
  2. Select the appointment procedure. Review the available options and choose the service that matches your needs.
  3. Enter the requested identification information. Have your DNI or NIE available and provide the details requested by the official system.
  4. Follow the instructions displayed on the screen. The available options and required information may vary according to the procedure.
  5. Review the appointment details. Confirm the information displayed before completing your request.
  6. Save your confirmation. Keep any reference number, appointment date, time, or other confirmation details provided by the system.

The exact screens and available appointment options may change, so follow the instructions currently displayed on the official website. If the system does not offer a suitable appointment, review the alternative procedures available through SEPE.

Can You Complete a SEPE Procedure Without an Appointment?

Yes, some procedures can be completed online without visiting a SEPE office. The official electronic office provides services for eligible users, including applications for unemployment benefits, benefit certificates, and consultations about benefit information.

Depending on the procedure, you may need an electronic certificate, an electronic DNI, or access through the Cl@ve identification system. Certain services also provide a presubmission form for users who do not have an electronic signature.

Before booking a visit, check whether your particular procedure can be completed online. This may save you time and help you avoid arranging an unnecessary appointment.

Official information is available through the SEPE electronic office.

SEPE Appointment by Phone: Official Telephone Number

Some people prefer requesting an appointment by telephone because they find it easier to follow spoken instructions or have difficulty using an online booking system.

The official SEPE telephone number for requesting an appointment is 91 926 79 70. According to SEPE's telephone information page, this appointment service is available 24 hours a day.

You can use the telephone appointment service to follow the automated instructions and request an appointment. Keep your identification details nearby and listen carefully to each instruction. If an appointment is confirmed, record the date, time, and any reference information provided.

For general information about unemployment benefits, administrative procedures, and benefit certificates, SEPE also lists 060 as its citizen information number. Calls to 060 may incur charges depending on your telephone operator and applicable tariff.

Always verify contact details through the official SEPE telephone service page. SEPE warns that telephone numbers beginning with 800 or 807 that claim to provide its services may involve special charges and are not official SEPE numbers.

What Should You Prepare Before Calling SEPE?

Before requesting an appointment by phone, prepare the following information where applicable:

  • Your DNI or NIE identification details.
  • Your full name and relevant personal information.
  • A clear explanation of the procedure you need.
  • Information about an existing unemployment benefit application, if relevant.
  • A pen or digital note-taking option to record appointment details.

Having this information available can make the telephone process more organized. If you are calling about an existing application, keep any relevant reference numbers or correspondence nearby.

How to Attend a SEPE Appointment in Person

An in-person appointment may be appropriate when you need assistance from a benefits office or when your particular procedure cannot be completed through another available method.

The appointment should correspond to the service you need, and you should attend the appropriate office according to the instructions provided during booking.

Before leaving home, review your confirmation and check the office location, appointment time, and identification requirements. Arriving prepared can help prevent problems during your visit.

Documents You May Need

The documents required depend on your personal circumstances and the procedure you are completing. Commonly relevant documents or details include:

  • DNI or NIE: Identification details used to verify your identity.
  • TIE or other applicable identification: Non-EU foreign nationals should check the specific identification requirements that apply to them.
  • Social Security information: Your Social Security number may be relevant to an employment or benefit procedure.
  • Employment documents: A company certificate, dismissal documentation, or other employment records may be needed depending on your case.
  • Bank account information: Spanish IBAN details may be required when the procedure involves benefit payments or banking information.
  • Application records: Previous correspondence, submission receipts, or benefit reference information can help explain your situation.

This list is a preparation guide rather than a universal document checklist. You should confirm the specific requirements for your procedure before attending.

SEPE also states that the name and DNI or NIE details must correspond to the appointment. The office checks identification, and non-EU foreign nationals may need to provide a TIE.

Registering as a Job Seeker Before Applying for Benefits

One important detail is that applying for unemployment benefits may require you to register as a job seeker before your SEPE appointment.

In most autonomous communities, job-seeker registration is handled by the regional public employment service rather than SEPE itself. Check the requirements of the employment service responsible for your area before attending your benefits appointment.

SEPE explains that, in certain circumstances, previous job-seeker registration within the preceding 180 days may allow registration to be completed automatically. If this condition does not apply to you or you have changed your address, you may need to contact the relevant regional employment service before attending the SEPE benefits office.

Checking this requirement in advance can help prevent delays with your application.

How to Choose the Right SEPE Appointment Method

The best method depends on the type of help you need, the identification options available to you, and whether an office visit is necessary.

Online booking is useful if you have internet access and want to arrange an appointment through the official electronic office. It is also worth checking whether your procedure can be completed online without an appointment.

Telephone booking may be more suitable if you prefer spoken instructions or need to use the appointment telephone service. The official appointment number is 91 926 79 70.

In-person assistance is appropriate when your procedure requires attention at a benefits office. In this case, make sure you have the correct appointment and the necessary identification documents.

Before selecting a method, identify the exact service you require. A question about an existing unemployment benefit may involve a different procedure from registering as a job seeker or renewing an employment demand.

Common SEPE Appointment Problems and How to Handle Them

Although the appointment process is designed to help people access services, users may sometimes encounter technical issues, limited availability, or confusion about the next step.

No Appointments Are Available

If the booking system does not display a suitable appointment, check the official website again and review the available options. You can also consult SEPE's official information about online procedures to determine whether your task can be completed electronically.

Do not assume that a particular office or date will become available at a specific time. Availability can change, and the booking system is the appropriate place to confirm current options.

The Online Booking System Is Not Working

If the website does not load correctly, check your internet connection and try again later. You may also try another browser or device if the problem appears to be technical.

Make sure you are using the official SEPE website and not an unofficial page that asks for unnecessary personal or payment information.

You Cannot Complete an Online Procedure

Some electronic procedures require a recognised electronic certificate, an electronic DNI, or Cl@ve access. If you cannot complete a procedure because you lack the required identification method, review the official alternatives for that specific task.

SEPE provides information about electronic services and presubmission procedures, but not every procedure is available through every access method.

You Have Lost Your Appointment Confirmation

If you cannot locate your confirmation, return to the official appointment service and check whether it provides an option to review or manage your booking. Keep any reference number or supporting message you received when arranging the appointment.

If you cannot find the information you need, consult the official SEPE contact channels rather than relying on unverified telephone numbers.

You Need to Change or Cancel Your Appointment

If you can no longer attend, review the official appointment system for the available cancellation options. The SEPE appointment service includes an option to cancel an appointment.

Follow the instructions displayed and keep any cancellation confirmation. If you still need assistance, arrange a new appointment when an appropriate option is available.

How to Prepare for an Unemployment Benefit Application

Preparing for an unemployment benefit application involves more than booking a SEPE appointment. You should understand the procedure, check the relevant eligibility requirements, and gather the documents associated with your employment situation.

Start by reviewing the official information about the benefit or subsidy you intend to request. Requirements may depend on your employment history, contribution record, reason for unemployment, and personal circumstances.

Next, check whether you need to register as a job seeker with your regional employment service. Complete that step first if it applies to your situation.

Organise your employment records, identification details, bank account information, and any supporting documents that may be relevant. Keep copies of application submissions and correspondence so that you can refer to them if questions arise.

Finally, check the applicable application deadline. Do not assume that booking an appointment automatically fulfils every application requirement or protects every deadline. If your deadline is approaching, consult official SEPE guidance promptly to understand which submission options are available.

Is SEPE the Same as the Regional Employment Service?

No. Although the two services work within Spain's public employment system, their responsibilities are different.

SEPE manages unemployment benefits and related administrative procedures at the state level. Regional public employment services generally manage job-seeker registration, employment demand renewals, job-search support, and many employment training services.

This distinction matters because contacting the wrong service can result in unnecessary delays. For example, if you need to register as a job seeker, you will generally need to consult the employment service of your autonomous community. If you need to ask about unemployment benefit recognition or payment, SEPE is the relevant authority.

Before booking, identify whether your question concerns an unemployment benefit, job-seeker registration, a training programme, or another employment service. Then consult the appropriate official website.

Conclusion

Arranging a SEPE appointment in 2026 can be easier when you understand the available booking methods and prepare the necessary information in advance. Whether you choose online booking, telephone assistance, or an in-person visit, the most important step is identifying the correct procedure and following the official instructions.

Before attending an appointment, check your confirmation, review the relevant document requirements, and make sure you understand whether prior registration as a job seeker is necessary. If you encounter booking problems, explore the official online services and contact options available for your situation.
